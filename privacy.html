Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. What we collect

Almost nothing. NovaMind AI has no accounts, no sign-ups, and no servers that store your conversations. Your chats stay in your browser and disappear when you close or reload the page.

2. Your API key (BYOK)

NovaMind works with your own API key ("bring your own key"). Your key is kept only in the page's temporary memory and is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you selected. We never see it, store it, or transmit it anywhere else. Closing or reloading the page wipes it.

3. Cookies

We use a minimal local-storage flag to remember that you accepted the cookie notice. Our advertising partner may use cookies to serve and measure ads (see below).

4. Third-party advertising — Adsterra

This site displays ads served by Adsterra. Adsterra may collect data such as your IP address, device type, and browsing activity to provide personalized or non-personalized advertising. This data is handled under Adsterra's own privacy policy. You can learn more and manage your choices via the cookie notice shown on your first visit.

5. Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly, aggregated analytics (such as Google Search Console performance data) to understand which pages appear in search results. This data is aggregated and cannot identify you personally.

6. Children's privacy

NovaMind AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Questions about this policy? Contact us via the site — we read every message.
